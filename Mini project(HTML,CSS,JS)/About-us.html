Introduction about my area

Like many urban areas, Anadura also struggles with various types of pollution that affect
its residents as well as the surrounding ecosystems. Air pollution from vehicle emissions and industrial
activities is a significant threat to public health, contributing to respiratory diseases and exacerbating climate change.
Water pollution, often caused by improper waste disposal and industrial waste, contaminates rivers and water
bodies essential for drinking water, agriculture and aquatic life. In addition, inadequate waste management
practices lead to littering and soil pollution, degrading the area's aesthetic appeal and harming biodiversity.
This website has been created to address these environmental challenges in Panadura by implementing sustainable practices,
improving infrastructure and raising awareness of the importance of protecting the environment for present and future generations.
